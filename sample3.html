Frequently Asked Questions

Clear advisory answers to guide your applications, document submissions, and visa journey.

What is Education Plus ISR and how does it support international students?
Education Plus ISR (International Student Recruitment) is our flagship division providing end-to-end university placement guidance across the UK, USA, Canada, and Australia—from initial academic profiling and course selection to certified document submissions and final unconditional offers.
How long does the university admissions and visa guidance process typically take?
Admissions timelines depend on university intake cycles (e.g. September/Fall or January/Spring intakes). Our advisory team reviews your initial profile within 24 to 48 hours to create a tailored timeline, ensuring that applications are completed 4 to 8 months ahead of departure.
Do you assist with student visa applications and compliance checks?
Yes. Our dedicated visa compliance desk conducts statutory documentation vetting, CAS and COE verification, proof-of-funds analysis, health surcharge guidance, and mock embassy interview preparation to maximize visa approval confidence.
What documents are required to start the preliminary profile assessment?
Typically, your recent academic transcripts, degree/graduation certificates, English language proficiency test scores (such as IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE, if available), a current CV, and your passport identification page.
Can I schedule a one-on-one consultation with an advisor?
Yes! You can complete the consultation form below or message our admissions desk directly on WhatsApp (+44 7495 359904) for prompt assessment within 24 to 48 hours.
