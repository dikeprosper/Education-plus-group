About Education Plus GroupOperating since 2010

Opening Doors. Creating Global Opportunities.

Education Plus Group is an education and advisory business committed to helping individuals access opportunities that can shape their futures. Through our flagship subsidiary, Education Plus International Student Recruitment, we support students seeking educational opportunities in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada and Australia.

We provide personalised guidance and practical support to help clients navigate course selection, admissions, English language requirements and relevant visa processes. Our approach is built around professionalism, transparency, inclusivity and a commitment to helping every client make informed decisions.

We understand that sending a student across oceans requires absolute certainty.

Any enrolled student aged 16 to 31 with a verified institutional email, student ID, or ISIC card qualifies for our contracted airfares. These fares include one to two additional 23kg checked bags at zero extra charge across major airline alliances (Star Alliance, SkyTeam, Oneworld) and allow flexible return-date shifts.
Most foreign universities and consular visa authorities (such as the Schengen Area or UK Home Office) require medical repatriation coverage of at least €30,000 / $50,000 with zero deductible. Voyageur & Co. policies satisfy 100% of these consular prerequisites and include 24/7 mental wellness lines, COVID hospital cover, and sport activity endorsements.
